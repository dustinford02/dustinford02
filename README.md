# Dustin S. Ford

Program manager and ServiceNow builder working where projects become
programs. I lead multi-vendor U.S. Department of Defense training and
simulation delivery, govern that work inside ServiceNow, and build on the
same platform to understand it from the inside out. Based in Florida,
United States.

## What I do

- Direct multi-vendor DoD training and simulation programs at the
  project-to-program interface, from award through delivery and government
  acceptance, with more than 15 years supporting the department.
- Govern delivery in ServiceNow PPM/SPM: schedule, cost, and KPI reporting;
  funding-risk mitigation; and portfolio visibility for government
  stakeholders.
- Turn program problems into platform solutions — using ServiceNow not just
  to report on the work, but to model and improve how the work runs.

## Featured work

**[Safety Issue Tracking — a scoped ServiceNow application](https://github.com/dustinford02/servicenow-safety-app)**

A scoped app built in App Engine Studio that demonstrates custom table
design and a role-based security model with per-operation, row-level, and
field-level access control. It ships with a written design rationale
covering the decisions made and the alternatives rejected, an importable
update set, and an honest account of what the work does and does not
demonstrate. An [overview site](https://dustinford02.github.io/servicenow-safety-app/)
is also published.
