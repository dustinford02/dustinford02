# Dustin S. Ford

Federal program and technical delivery manager. I run multi-vendor
DoD training and technology programs at the project-to-program
interface, and I govern that delivery inside ServiceNow PPM/SPM —
CLIN-level burn and EAC forecasting, funding-risk mitigation, schedule
and KPI reporting to government stakeholders. This account is where I
build on the same platform I manage in.

Retired U.S. Navy Chief Air Traffic Controller. PMP. Active Top Secret
clearance. Ed.D. candidate in Instructional and Performance Technology
at the University of West Florida, researching human performance
technology and AI.

## Featured project

[Safety Issue Tracking: A Scoped ServiceNow Application](https://github.com/dustinford02/servicenow-safety-app)

A scoped app built in App Engine Studio demonstrating custom table
design and a role-based security model with per-operation, row-level,
and field-level access control. Includes a written design rationale
covering the decisions made and the alternatives rejected, an
importable update set, and an honest account of what the work does
not demonstrate.
